# Sky Highway — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-04_

**Sky Highway** ("the game") is an offline arcade game published by **[YOUR COMPANY /
NAME]** ("we"). This policy describes what information the game handles.

## What we collect

**Nothing, ourselves.** The game has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of our own.
All progress (levels, coins, streaks, settings, replays) is stored **only on your device**
in local app storage and never transmitted to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

## Third-party services

The game shows advertising through **Google AdMob** (Google LLC). AdMob may collect and
process device information (such as advertising identifiers, IP address, and coarse
device data) to serve and measure ads, as described in
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[how Google uses information from partner apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

- On iOS, personalized advertising is only enabled if you allow tracking via Apple's
  App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can decline; ads still work, non-personalized.
- On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of personalization
  in system settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
- Purchasing **Premium** removes banner and interstitial ads entirely; rewarded ads only
  play when you explicitly choose to watch one.

In-app purchases are processed by Apple App Store / Google Play. We never see your
payment details.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Ad content is limited to Google's "T"
(Teen) maximum ad content rating and child-directed treatment tags are not set.

## Notifications

Optional local reminders (e.g. daily-challenge streaks) are scheduled on your device
only, and only after you grant the notification permission. No push servers are involved.
