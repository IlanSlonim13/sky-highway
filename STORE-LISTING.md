## Full description
```
SKY HIGHWAY is a retro-futuristic arcade runner. Pilot a neon ship down highways
suspended in space — jump the gaps, weave the towers, blast through barriers, and
thread glowing rings at the top of your arc.

MASTER TWO JUMPS
Tap to hop a low block. HOLD to soar over energy fences and wide chasms. Duck UNDER
floating space debris. Every level is built to demand both — timing is everything.

RACE YOUR OWN PAST
Every run is recorded. Your best ghost — your "echo" — flies right beside you. Beat it
to the finish for a bonus. Crash and your last attempt joins the next try. It's you
versus you.

THREE WAYS TO PLAY
• CAMPAIGN — 500 levels that grow longer and wilder as you climb.
• TODAY'S RUN — one daily track the entire world shares. Keep your streak alive.
• HYPERDRIVE — an endless track that never stops speeding up.

PROGRESS EVERY SESSION
Rank up your pilot, chase 3 stars on every level, spin the daily prize wheel, complete
missions, and unlock ships and trails in the Hangar.

FEEL-GOOD EXTRAS
• Slow-motion to thread the tightest gaps
• A 3-second rewind that gives crashed runs a second chance
• Left-handed mode and one-thumb controls
• Plays offline — your progress lives on your device

Free to play. Optional ads and in-app purchases. Remove ads any time with Premium.
```
